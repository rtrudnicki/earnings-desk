Company overview

Tue Oct 20

Space Exploration Technologies (SpaceX): Falcon and Starship launch, the Starlink satellite network (consumer, enterprise, government/Starshield, mobile), and an AI segment (Grok, X advertising, Colossus compute sold under Cloud Services Agreements, Cursor since 8/14/2026). Listed 6/12/2026 on Nasdaq Global Select and Nasdaq Texas; IPO of 638.9M Class A shares for ~$85.7B net proceeds. This is its second report as a public company (first: Q2 on 8/4/2026)

How it makes money. Q2 2026 revenue $7.81B: Connectivity $4.29B (~55%; consumer $2.49B, enterprise and government $1.81B), AI $2.56B (~33%; AI solutions and infrastructure $2.19B, advertising $0.37B), Space $0.96B (~12%; launch services $0.65B, launch and development $0.31B). Only Connectivity makes an operating profit ($1.66B); Space -$0.54B, AI -$1.26B

Bull case

Analysis: Starlink is a cash engine (Connectivity Adjusted EBITDA $2.6B a quarter, subscribers doubled to 12.0M) funding Starship and AI; the AI segment flipped to positive Adjusted EBITDA ($1.1B) on $14.1B of Cloud Services contracts, and management targets $100B of annualized revenue by December 2026

Bear case

Analysis: ~$2.0T market cap on ~$45B of 2026 Street revenue (~44x sales); capex ran $18.4B in Q2, 86% of it AI, against a $1.9B a quarter and rising AI depreciation bill; Starlink ARPU fell from $85 to $66 a year; lockup tranches keep adding supply into December

Our questions for management

  1. The $100B December run rate: how much of it is Cloud Services compute, Cursor and Starlink, and how much of the $14.1B contracted in Q2 has turned into revenue so far?
  2. Capex near $18B a quarter is mostly AI. What payback do you underwrite on a GW of compute, how much of the 2 GW is under contract, and what does 2027 capex look like against the 10 GW goal?
  3. Starlink ARPU fell from $85 to $66 in a year. Where does average ARPU settle as international and mobile grow, and when do V3 satellites add enough capacity to lift it?
