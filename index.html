The read

The swing. Core PCE month over month at 07:30 CT: 0.2% or below lets yields fall and growth stocks recover; 0.4% or above adds a rates shock to the AI-spending shock.

  • Alphabet is down about 10% premarket after free cash flow swung to negative $9.07 billion on $51.4 billion of capital spending, while Microsoft is up 3% to 4% on 48% Azure growth and $18.6 billion of free cash flow; Nasdaq-100 futures are down about 1%, S&P futures down 0.3%, Dow futures up 0.3%.
  • The Fed held at 3.75% to 4.00% in a 10-2 vote with Cook and Hammack dissenting for a hike, upgraded activity to 'expanding at a strong pace,' and markets now price roughly 83% to 85% odds of a December hike.
  • The 10-year yield is near 5.00% after 5.02% to 5.04% on Fed day, and a $44 billion 7-year auction lands at 12:00 CT a month after the highest 7-year auction yield since 1993.
  • Brent is near $113 to $114, down about 1.7%, on reports of Vice President Vance's outreach to Iran, after settling at $115.32 on Wednesday; oil is the swing for headline PCE and the Fed's inflation read.
  • Bank of Canada hiked 25 bp to 2.50% on Wednesday, the ECB is expected to hold at 2.50% at 08:15 CT, and the Bank of Japan decides overnight, so the global direction of travel is tighter policy into high energy prices.

Pay attention to

  1. The 07:30 CT core PCE month-over-month print against 0.3%: it is the only number today that can move December hike odds, which sit near 85%, in either direction.
  2. The GDP split between consumer spending, business investment and net exports: a 3%-plus headline built on AI equipment imports and inventories reads differently from one built on the consumer.
  3. The ECB decision at 08:15 CT and Lagarde at 08:45 CT: a hold is priced, so the trade is in whether she commits to December with euro area inflation at 3.8% and sentiment just missing.
  4. Nasdaq breadth after Alphabet: whether chip names and other capex-heavy AI stocks follow it down or Microsoft's cash-flow story holds the group, and whether the Dow keeps outperforming.
  5. The 12:00 CT $44 billion 7-year auction with the 10-year at 5.00%: a weak tail after last month's 5.085% stop would push long yields higher into Amazon's results after the close and the Bank of Japan decision overnight at 22:00 CT, where a hold at 1.25% is expected.
