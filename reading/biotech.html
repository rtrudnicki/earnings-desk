One level deeper

What a partial clinical hold over a rodent metabolite actually means (Biohaven's opakalim)
A partial clinical hold under 21 CFR 312.42 stops part of a program, here new enrollment, while patients already dosed continue, because FDA judged the incremental risk of a new exposure differently from the risk of stopping treatment in an epilepsy cohort that is already stable on drug. The trigger is a metabolite seen in rodents whose relevance to humans is 'uncertain', which inverts the usual MIST question: ICH M3(R2) asks whether human metabolites above about 10% of total drug-related exposure are covered by the toxicology species, whereas here the species produced something and the sponsor must show humans do not, or do so at exposures the existing tox package already covers. That is why 'nonclinical data in the coming weeks' is plausible: in vitro hepatocyte and plasma profiling, cross-species exposure ratios and a re-read of stored human PK samples take weeks, while a new dedicated toxicology study would take months and the company would not say weeks. Two things to check rather than assume. First, the letter is dated September 4 and surfaced in the October 29 release; confirm whether an earlier 8-K carried it, because a 55-day lag to disclosure on the asset being sold is a governance datapoint in itself. Second, read the SK Biopharm agreement's closing conditions: HSR clearance is done, so what remains is representations, warranties and any material-adverse-change clause, and a hold on the licensed asset is exactly what those clauses are written for. Finally, a hold on enrollment does not invalidate RISE 3, which was fully enrolled before the letter, so a positive 2H 2026 topline can still arrive while the hold stands; the label consequence would be whether FDA asks for the metabolite work to be closed before accepting an NDA, which is a timing cost, not a data cost. The analyst move is to price the hold as a closing-risk and timing-risk event with a bimodal resolution in weeks, and to resist reading the 25% stock move on the Vyglxia acceptance as the market having already done that work.
