The read

The swing. A tariff headline: the administration's proposed new duties on 14 to 16 trading partners set off Wednesday's chip-led plunge, and any detail on rates or targets in a market with no cash session would move futures more than any scheduled item.

  • No US data and no Fed speakers today; stock-index and Treasury futures stop at 07:00 CT, crude and metals at 08:30 CT.
  • Wednesday's close: S&P 500 7,182.78 (-0.25%) after an intraday low of 6,970.11, Nasdaq Composite 23,585.80 (-0.62%) after being down 4.7%, Dow 50,182.95 (+0.33%); VIX closed at 28.03 after touching about 35.
  • October core PCE came in at 3.3% y/y against 3.2% expected, the highest in three years, and a December Fed hike is priced at roughly 94% to 98%.
  • Rates fell anyway on the equity scare: 10-year yield 4.81% (-9 bp), 2-year 4.59% (-8 bp); a $44bn 7-year auction tailed 1.0 bp.
  • One report that could not be opened (the page returned an error) had S&P 500 futures up about 0.4% near 7,225 early Thursday; treat that level as unverified.

Pay attention to

  1. Tariff headlines first: the proposed duties on 14 to 16 trading partners have no confirmed rates yet, and any detail lands on a market with no US cash session to absorb it.
  2. The ECB meeting account at 06:30 CT, the only scheduled item before US index futures stop at 07:00 CT, for how seriously the ECB is discussing tighter policy.
  3. Where S&P 500 futures sit against Wednesday's 7,182.78 close and the 6,970 intraday low when they halt at 07:00 CT; that gap sets the tone for Friday's half session (stocks close 12:00 CT, bonds 13:00 CT).
  4. Tokyo CPI this evening (core seen at 3.0% from 2.6%) with the yen near 159 per dollar; a hot number pushes global yields up into Friday.
  5. The weekend OPEC+ meeting and the Dec 4 jobs report are the next real tests before the Fed decides on Dec 9.
