One level deeper

Why a wet AMD non-inferiority trial can fail twice while the drug 'works' (EyePoint LUCIA)
A non-inferiority trial does not ask whether the drug improves vision; it asks whether the lower bound of the confidence interval for the difference versus the active comparator stays inside a pre-agreed margin, here minus 4.5 letters of BCVA against aflibercept 2 mg every 8 weeks, averaged over weeks 52 and 56. Two things decide that bound: the point estimate and the width of the interval, and the width is set by sample size and by variance in the BCVA measurement. LUGANO's full-population difference was reported as 3.8 letters, which already consumes most of the 4.5-letter margin, so even modest variance pushes the lower bound through it; EyePoint's post hoc exclusion of nine patients with non-AMD vision loss moved the estimate to 2.4 letters and 'rescued' NI, but the FDA treats a post hoc exclusion as hypothesis-generating because the exclusion rule was not pre-specified and the patients were not balanced by design. The trap for an analyst is the secondary endpoints: a 38% to 42% reduction in injection burden and more than half of patients supplement-free are real, but they describe a rescue-heavy design in which supplemental aflibercept is doing part of the work, and the primary BCVA comparison is what the label will say. The second trap is dose: a 2.7 mg insert dosed every 24 weeks is a fixed exposure choice made before Phase 3, and two identical misses are better explained by under-dosing in the back half of the interval than by chance. Compare Disc's APOLLO the same morning: after a CRL, Disc agreed co-primary endpoints with the FDA at a Type A meeting (a biomarker, PPIX, paired with a clinical measure, pain-free hours) so that a win on both left no interpretive gap. When you underwrite an NI trial, write down the margin, the comparator's expected effect in the trial population, the planned n and the assumed SD, then ask how much of the margin the Phase 2 point estimate already spends; if it is more than half, the Phase 3 PoS is lower than the Phase 2 headline suggests.
