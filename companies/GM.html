Surprise and drivers

The beat came from Revenue, Margin, Share count.

Organic
GMNA wholesale 870k vs 840k (+3.6%); GMI wholesale 159k vs 137k. U.S. total sales 671k vs 710k (-5.5%), share 16.1% vs 16.8%; U.S. trucks 359k vs 369k, crossovers 300k vs 330k. Fleet share of total sales 19.2% vs 16.7%. China JV sales 358k vs 469k (-24%) (8-K Ex 99.1, vehicle sales tables). Call: full-size pickup sales up 9% (gloom.sh summary). Analysis: revenue growth came from wholesale timing and mix, not retail demand; U.S. retail volume and share fell and fleet mix rose.
Margin
Call: GMNA gains from richer full-size pickup mix, lower EV losses and lower warranty, offset by commodities (including logistics and DRAM) and onshoring costs. Commodities, logistics and DRAM about $700M in Q3, about $1.3B YTD, about $2B for FY26. EV losses expected down $1B to $1.5B for FY26. NA pricing up $400M YTD (Investing.com transcript; Yahoo Finance call summary). Ultium Cells equity income was insignificant vs $213M a year ago (8-K footnote b).
One-time
Excluded from adjusted: EV strategic realignment $238M pre-tax (vs $1.592B a year ago) and a $208M tax benefit from a change to a subsidiary's legal entity tax classification, so GAAP EPS ($3.73) is above adjusted ($3.70) (8-K Ex 99.1, EPS reconciliation). Inside adjusted: GMNA equity income includes a gain from the sale of an equity investment, amount not disclosed (8-K footnote c); the call cited a $600M Q3 warranty benefit and about $500M of IEEPA tariff refunds credited to earnings, with the quarter split of the refund not stated in the sources read; about $100M negative Momenta mark in GMI (Investing.com transcript; gloom.sh summary). Analysis: warranty and tariff refunds are part of the beat and the company says the refund will not repeat in 2027.
Cash conversion
Automotive operating cash flow $8.049B; capex $2.333B; adjusted automotive FCF $5.882B, about 143% of EBIT-adjusted. YTD adjusted FCF $12.184B, with $4.242B of EV realignment cash added back (8-K Ex 99.1). Call: Q3 FCF helped by tariff timing. Automotive cash $18.765B plus marketable securities $4.351B at Sep 30 (8-K balance sheet).
Share count and tax
Diluted weighted shares 887M vs 910M in Q2 and 964M a year ago; 849M shares outstanding at Sep 30 vs 904M at end 2025 (8-K Ex 99.1). Buyback $2.5B in Q3, $1B more in October, $6.3B YTD, prior $6.0B program completed; new $20B multi-year authorization (release; call). ETR-adjusted 19.9% vs 19.8% (8-K). Analysis: at Q2's 910M diluted shares, adjusted EPS would have been about $3.60, so share count added about $0.10.
Segments
GMNA EBIT-adj $3.592B (+43.4%) on revenue $41.419B (+2.1%); GMI EBIT-adj $28M vs $226M (-87.7%), including China equity income $32M vs $80M; GM Financial EBT-adj $668M vs $804M (-16.9%); Corporate and eliminations -$183M vs -$160M (8-K Ex 99.1, segment reconciliation). Per the call, GMI ex-China was break-even and included about a $100M negative mark-to-market on Momenta (Investing.com transcript).
