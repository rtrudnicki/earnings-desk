No named bottleneck components and no supplier co-investment commitment.
New information
Preliminary 2027 WFE above $200B; 2026 WFE raised to mid $150B; 2026 systems revenue growth 'well over 40%'; CSBG growth above 30% in 2026 and above 15% in 2027; some December revenue moves to March on supply; China down meaningfully in December; tax 16.5% continuing; NAND up about 40% Q/Q with early 384-layer spending; Aether about $2.5B by decade-end; ASR under way with about $3B authorization left; inventory turns 3.4x (Stifel question). China 'meaningfully down' in December with no number; mid-50s gross margin 'over the next several years', near term 'bounce around generally where it is', with new products, close-to-customer manufacturing and pricing as the levers; tax 16.5% (Call transcript (Investing.com, AI-assisted, 10/21/2026)).
