The read

The swing. The 10:00 CT NY Fed one-year inflation expectation: another rise from 3.9% the day before CPI pushes the hike toward a lock, a fall gives the front end room to breathe.

  • Wholesale inventories were revised up to 0.7% m/m with sales up 2.8% and the stock-to-sales ratio down to 1.21 months, a growth-positive mix for Q4 tracking.
  • The Employment Trends Index held near a two-year high at 108.36 with September revised up almost a full point, confirming Friday's 165k payrolls.
  • Goolsbee said services inflation is kicking up and the data-center buildout looks like classic overheating, the first Fed voice since the jobs report and a hawkish one.
  • Oil fell about 3% to roughly $88 WTI on Iran diplomacy hopes, the one disinflationary input today, and stocks rallied 1.6-2.5% off last week's worst drawdown since April 2025.
  • The 10-year sits near 4.87% and the 3-year note auction at 12:00 CT is the first test of coupon demand at these levels before CPI.

Pay attention to

  1. NY Fed one-year inflation expectation at 10:00 CT (prior 3.9%, the highest since May 2023): a further rise the day before CPI would move December hike odds from 80% toward a lock.
  2. The 3-year note auction at 12:00 CT (prior stop 4.932%): a tail at these yields says the front end wants a second hike priced, a strong bid says one and done.
  3. GDPNow after the wholesale data: 3.4% on November 4, and today's upward inventory revision leans toward a higher print.
  4. Barkin at 11:15 CT and Goolsbee's second appearance at 14:00 CT: whether either puts a number on December after this morning's services-inflation remarks.
  5. Oil: the roughly 3% slide to $88 WTI is the only disinflationary input today; a reversal on Iran headlines would take the equity rally with it.
