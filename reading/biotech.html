One level deeper

How a pneumococcal vaccine 'wins' a noninferiority trial, and why serotypes 3 and 12F in OPUS-1 are the whole FDA story
Vaccine pivotal trials do not measure disease. They measure a surrogate, here the opsonophagocytic activity (OPA) titer for each serotype, and compare the geometric mean titer of the new vaccine to the comparator's as a ratio (GMR). Noninferiority is declared per serotype when the lower bound of the two-sided 95% confidence interval on that ratio clears a pre-specified margin. The margin is a convention, not biology: the FDA licensed PCV20 and PCV21 against a GMR lower bound of 0.5 (the new vaccine may be no worse than half the comparator's titer). Vaxcyte pre-specified 0.667 (no worse than two-thirds) and then told you it met noninferiority at that bar on all 28 shared serotypes against the pooled comparators. That is the achievement in a 31-valent product, because every added conjugate competes for the same carrier protein and the same immune machinery, so historically more serotypes meant weaker responses on the shared ones. The trap is in the per-comparator cut. Against PCV21 alone, serotypes 3 and 12F missed 0.667 and only cleared 0.5. Serotype 3 is the weakest immunogen in every conjugate vaccine ever licensed and is also the serotype still causing most adult invasive disease, so a reviewer who already discounts its surrogate will look hard at it. The company's own line that the PCV21 comparison is 'expected' to be 'a particular focus of FDA review' is management pre-negotiating that discussion in public. Two lessons for reading any vaccine or biosimilar immunogenicity trial: first, ask what margin was pre-specified and whether the press release's 'all endpoints met' statement would still be true at the margin the regulator actually used (here it is, which is why this is a label debate and not an approvability one); second, separate the pooled comparison from each comparator, because the label will be written against the product the FDA considers the relevant standard in that age group, and in adults 50-plus that is now Merck's PCV21, not Pfizer's PCV20. The 2028 BLA timing means those two serotypes will be relitigated with OPUS-2 and OPUS-3 data in 1H 2027 before any of it reaches a reviewer.
