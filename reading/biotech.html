At the Jefferies London conference this morning (Nov 18), CEO Kabir Nath said the rolling NDA was completed, with the article dating the filing to Nov 9. Management expects FDA action within one to two months of completion under its Commissioner's National Priority Voucher, expects a PDUFA date to be assigned soon, and does not expect an advisory committee; the moderator framed the window as Dec 9 to Jan 9. After approval the DEA scheduling step could take about 90 days (Schedule III expected), and states covering about 93% of the US population have committed to reschedule within 30 days of DEA action. Launch plan: about 100 reps, roughly 8,500 SPRAVATO-certified sites growing about 500 a quarter, price at a premium to SPRAVATO's $50,000 to $65,000 a year on 2 to 4 doses a year. The article quotes the stock at $7.98 and a $1.13B market cap. No cash update was given.
The whole 1H 2027 launch rests on the voucher clock. Reuters reported on Nov 12, citing six sources and internal documents, that FDA has shelved the CNPV pilot: no new vouchers since Makary left in May and the oversight council inactive. HHS says career staff continue to meet commitments on vouchers already granted, and the tally so far is 22 vouchers, 12 approvals, 1 rejection. Read: if COMP360 is honored as a grandfathered voucher, approval lands by early January and the DEA plus state steps (about 4 months) put first scripts around May 2027. If it reverts to a normal priority review, the goal date is about 8 months from submission, so roughly July 2027, and launch slides to Q4 2027. Separately, Helus reported a Phase 3 win for its deuterated psilocybin analog in adjunctive MDD on Nov 16 (see sector pulse), the first late-stage competitor data in the class.
Thesis read: Your one-liner still names the Phase 3 readout as the binary. That is behind you; the live binary is review timing, then approval, then scheduling. The stock at about $7.98 sits roughly 21% under your $10.08 mark and 23% under your $10.43 rNPV, which reads as the market pricing a slip in the voucher timeline rather than an efficacy doubt. The tell is the PDUFA letter: a goal date inside January confirms the fast path, a date in mid-2027 means the 1H27 launch assumption in the model needs to move two quarters. No trim trigger is near.

One level deeper

Why a statistically significant foreign trial can still draw a CRL, and what that means for telitacicept
Summit's HARMONi trial hit its PFS endpoint and FDA still refused the application, for two separate reasons you should keep apart. First, the endpoint hierarchy: in a second-line lung cancer setting with existing options, FDA treats PFS as supportive and wants overall survival at least trending clearly the right way, and HARMONi's OS missed significance. Second, applicability: 93% of the PFS events came from patients in China, most of them rolled over from Akeso's single-country HARMONi-A study, so the estimate of benefit is an estimate for Chinese patients treated under Chinese standards of care. Under 21 CFR 314.106, foreign data can support approval alone only if it is applicable to the US population and US medical practice, and FDA has enforced that since the 2022 sintilimab advisory committee. The differences that matter are concrete: prior therapy patterns, what the control arm receives, genetics that change drug metabolism or tumor biology, and how intensively sites follow patients. The trap for an analyst is treating a China Phase 3 effect size as the base case for the global trial. Vor told investors yesterday to expect more than 2 points of placebo-adjusted MG-ADL benefit in its global gMG study against 4.8 in China, and gave the reason: older patients with longer disease duration. That is the right way to handicap it. When you model a China-originated asset, start from the Western comparator's effect size and ask what evidence justifies moving above it, rather than starting from the China number and discounting by feel.
