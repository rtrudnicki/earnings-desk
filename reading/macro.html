Overnight: Two AI spenders reported negative free cash flow in the same week and the market stopped paying for it: Alphabet lost 12.6% on Thursday (its worst day since listing) and Amazon followed down 7% after hours, then Intel guided Q4 well under consensus and lost a fifth of its value. That is the whole story in the Nasdaq futures gap and the Nikkei's chip-led 3% drop; the Dow and the non-tech tape were higher on Thursday. Two snapshot rows need care: the 10Y and 30Y yield values are a feed error (the 10Y actually closed near 4.98% after touching 5.05% Wednesday), and the WTI-up, Brent-down split is the December Brent contract expiring today near $116 against January near $112, not a fundamental divergence.

What mattered

Tech
Alphabet and Amazon both burn cash on AI and the market now charges for it
Alphabet closed at $283.82, down 12.6%, erasing about $500B, after Q3 capex of $51.4B pushed free cash flow to -$9.07B (Street expected +$2.7B) with cloud revenue up 103% to $30.7B and backlog $561B; management held 2026 capex at $195-205B and said 2027 spend will increase significantly. Amazon then printed EPS $1.89 vs $1.96 expected on revenue of $201.9B (+12%), AWS +39% to about $46B, record 13.9% operating margin, free cash flow -$42.4B, 2026 capex held at $220B, Q4 revenue guide $240-247B; shares fell about 7% after hours.
So what: The regime has flipped from rewarding capex to pricing it as a drain. Microsoft, which spent $45.2B against its own above-$50B outlook and still grew Azure 48%, rose; the two that overspent fell. Read-through: equipment and power suppliers (Advantest and Tokyo Electron led Tokyo lower, Caterpillar sold off on a narrow beat) now trade on hyperscaler cash flow, not hyperscaler intent. Jassy's call for AWS above 40% growth in 2027 and 2028 is the bull case; the bear case is that the market will not finance it at a 5% 10Y.

Tech
Intel loses a fifth of its value on a Q4 guide that says the PC cycle is rolling over
Q3 revenue $16.5B (+21%) and non-GAAP EPS $0.52 beat (about $0.40 expected), but Intel guided Q4 revenue to $15.0-16.0B and EPS to $0.25 against consensus of about $17.05B and $0.43. CFO Zinsner said the PC slowdown runs at least through 1H27. The guide includes roughly $300M of revenue and 200bp of gross margin lost to an incident at Fab 28 in Israel. Shares fell from $92.07 to $72.85 after hours, -20.9%.
So what: Second consumer-electronics demand warning of the season after Alphabet's slower search growth. A PC downturn through mid-2027 hits AMD, Micron, and the Taiwan supply chain on volume, and the Fab 28 disruption is a reminder that Israel-based capacity carries the same geopolitical premium as Hormuz crude.

Rates
Three central banks held this week and all three left a hike on the table
The Fed held at 3.75-4.00% on a 10-2 vote with Cook and Hammack dissenting for a 25bp hike; the statement called activity strong and inflation elevated. Q3 GDP printed 3.4% annualised vs about 3.2% expected and September PCE was 3.6% y/y headline, 3.1% core. The ECB held the deposit rate at 2.50%, said it discussed a hike, and markets price a 67-82% chance of one in December; Lagarde will leave on 7 February 2027. The BOJ held at 1.25% on an 8-1 vote and said underlying inflation has reached about 2%. The 10Y touched 5.05% Wednesday and closed near 4.98% Thursday; gold fell toward $4,000.
So what: The hiking bias is global, and it lands on the same week the two largest capex spenders went FCF-negative. Duration and cash burn are the two things a 5% discount rate punishes most, which is why the equal-weight index is holding and the Nasdaq is not. Watch the 2s10s at about 37bp: a bear flattener from here says the market believes the dissenters.

Credit
Binance loses $6.3B in the largest crypto theft on record
Binance said about $6.3B was affected; roughly $3B (Binance) to $4.2B (ABC) has been frozen on-chain, and 18,270 BTC worth about $1.4B sits in 19 wallets nobody can freeze. The company's early read is a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop giving access to a service container; TRM Labs attributes it to North Korea's TraderTraitor with high confidence. The freezes by Tether and Circle that saved most of the stolen stablecoins also prove the issuers can and will halt funds on request, which cuts both ways for the stablecoin-rails story Mastercard was selling the same day. Coinbase and the ETF complex pick up flight-to-regulated flows; Binance-adjacent tokens and perps venues lose them.

Energy

Take: Crude is still trading the Hormuz war premium: December Brent expires today near $116 with January near $112, so the WTI-up, Brent-down split in the snapshot is a roll artifact. Fresh Houthi claims on Saudi refineries and a US back-channel to Tehran pulled in opposite directions overnight; the Vance letter is the new variable.
Geopolitics
Houthis claim strikes on Ras Tanura, Rabigh, Abqaiq and the East-West pipeline
The Houthis said Thursday they hit Saudi Arabia's largest refinery at Ras Tanura, the Rabigh refinery, an Aramco facility at Abqaiq and the East-West pipeline that carries crude to the Red Sea around Hormuz; the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a cruise missile and ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh and the Eastern Province. Riyadh has not confirmed damage. Separately, Iran's Revolutionary Guards said three tankers struck mines on an unauthorised route south of the strait.
So what: The East-West pipeline is the Hormuz bypass; a confirmed hit there removes the one supply route the market has been treating as safe and would reprice the Brent-Dubai spread wider. Unconfirmed claims are the norm in this theatre, so treat the overnight bid as a hedge, not a fact.
https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/oil-rises-on-supply-concerns-as-houthis-attack-saudi-arabi
Geopolitics
Vance letter to Tehran opens a talks channel Trump says he did not order
The Wall Street Journal reported Vance wrote to Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, to revive peace talks; Trump said he had not directed it and Tasnim says Tehran has received it but not responded. December Brent slipped 1.2% to $113.91 on the report Thursday before firming Friday.
So what: This is the first downside catalyst for crude in weeks. If Tehran engages, the $108 bear case on Brent is in play and the refiners' margin windfall compresses; if the letter is disowned, the premium stays. RUMOR-grade on the administration's intent: one newspaper, one unnamed-source story, with the president contradicting it on the record.
https://qz.com/oil-prices-vance-letter-iran-peace-talks-102926

Natural gas

Take: Henry Hub is sinking under a surplus: Thursday's injection beat, storage is above the five-year average, output is at record highs, and the forecast is mild into mid-November. The December contract is defending $2.90 and the El Niño winter view is doing the rest.
Storage
80 Bcf injection beats, stocks 38 Bcf above the five-year average
EIA reported an 80 Bcf build for the week ended 23 October, taking working gas to 3,724 Bcf, 38 Bcf above the five-year average and above the 77 Bcf build the prior week. Lower 48 output averaged 112.1 Bcf/d in October and LNG feedgas about 19.1 Bcf/d over the past two weeks. EIA's October STEO sees end-October storage near 3,850 Bcf, about 2% above normal.
So what: At this pace the market enters withdrawal season with a cushion, so any November cold needs to be sustained, not a single front, to lift the strip. Producers with hedges off (Appalachian gas names) feel it first; LNG exporters are indifferent since feedgas is already at capacity.
https://naturalgasintel.com/

Data centers

Take: The build-out did not slow this week; its financing terms did. Capex guides from Alphabet ($195-205B, up significantly in 2027), Amazon ($220B held, no 2027 number) and Microsoft ($45.2B quarter) are all at or above prior plan, but the equity market is no longer paying for the spend, which raises the cost of capital for everything downstream of it.
Capex
Three hyperscaler guides in 48 hours: spend holds, equity funding gets expensive
Alphabet Q3 capex $51.4B, 2026 guide $195-205B held, 2027 to increase significantly; Amazon 2026 guide $220B held with no 2027 figure (Evercore ISI models $320B); Microsoft $45.2B in the quarter against an above-$50B outlook. Alphabet and Amazon both printed negative free cash flow; Alphabet lost about $500B of market value in a day.
So what: Expect more of the Microsoft pattern (leases, off-balance-sheet neoclouds, vendor financing) and less balance-sheet capex. That shifts risk to the Oracle/CoreWeave/private-credit layer and to the gas-turbine and transformer vendors whose backlogs assume the spend arrives on time.
https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/29/amazon-amzn-q3-earnings-report-2026.html
Trend
SemiAnalysis sees 51 GW of net new IT capacity outside China in 2027
A 29 October report projects 51.2 GW of net new data-center IT capacity ex-China in 2027, more than double its 2026 estimate, with over 60 GW under construction; nearly 12 GW of the 2027 figure is projects still classed as planned, not started.
So what: Twelve of the 51 GW is the part that this week's repricing puts at risk. Power procurement, not chips, is the binding constraint at that scale; the 2027 number only lands if the gas-plant filings below clear state regulators on time.
