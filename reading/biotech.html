CMPS
COMP360 NDA completed Nov 9, eight days ahead of your Nov 17 row
Compass said on Nov 9 that it completed the rolling NDA for COMP360 psilocybin in treatment-resistant depression. The package rests on two positive Phase 3 trials (COMP005, COMP006) plus the Phase 2b, more than 1,000 participants in total. The company holds a Commissioner's National Priority Voucher, which targets a 1-2 month review, and repeats launch readiness for 1H 2027. No filing acceptance, review date or PDUFA date has been published.
Management said on the Q3 call that the 1-2 month voucher goal starts at NDA completion, so the earliest decision window is roughly Dec 9 to Jan 9 if FDA holds to it. FDA has not committed to that clock and the voucher does not guarantee it. Approval alone does not permit sales: COMP360 is Schedule I, so DEA must reschedule after approval and each state must follow; the company says a set of states reschedule within 30 days of federal action. The voucher changes FDA timing, not DEA timing.
Thesis read: Read: this pulls the timeline in but changes nothing about the data, so by your own rule it is not a fundamental change. If the stock runs to $14-15 on the filing headline, the trim applies. The next real information is FDA acceptance and any stated action date; the seven duplicate Nov 17 NDA rows in your table are now done and should close.

CATX
Q3: BMS ends the VMT01 plus nivolumab collaboration; melanoma is now a monotherapy program with a mid-2027 read
Perspective reported Q3 on Nov 9: net loss $32.4M ($0.27 per share) vs $26.0M, R&D $26.1M vs $20.3M, cash about $202M vs about $145M at year-end 2025. The 10-Q discloses that Bristol Myers Squibb is terminating the VMT01 plus nivolumab clinical collaboration; secondary coverage of the filing cites "economic factors" and an orderly wind-down (I could not open the release itself, so that wording is second-hand). VMT01 3.0 mCi monotherapy: 12 patients treated as of Oct 30, five since the cohort reopened in Q3, expected to reach 24 weeks of follow-up by mid-2027. Why it matters: The two VMT01 rows your table had at Dec 1 and Dec 31, 2026 are gone; the replacement is a five-patient monotherapy follow-up in mid-2027. Disease control in heavily pretreated melanoma is a low bar; response rate and duration are what a registrational case needs. The value of the company stays on VMT-alpha-NET: the Nov 20 ENA poster and the late-2026 Cohort 2 60-week durability cut. Around the industry

FDA
Class-wide NAION warning ordered for all 12 marketed GLP-1 receptor agonists
FDA's Nov 9 safety communication requires a Warnings and Precautions update (not a boxed warning) for non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy across the class, including semaglutide, tirzepatide and orforglipron. FDA's meta-analysis of 126 randomized trials found 15 probable NAION events, 10 on GLP-1 drugs and 5 on placebo; its animal review found no mechanism. Novo disputes causality.
Read-through: Read: ten events across 126 trials will not change prescribing. Because orforglipron is included, small-molecule GLP-1s inherit the language, so expect it on any aleniglipron (GPCR) or ribupatide (KLRA) label and ophthalmic adverse-event collection in their Phase 3 programs.

Bayer's asundexian (Sunharbo) approved as the first factor XIa inhibitor
Approved Nov 9 for secondary prevention after non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke (NIHSS 15 or below) or high-risk TIA, on top of antiplatelets. Why hidradenitis suppurativa trials fail on the placebo arm, and what to check before trusting an HS readout
Zura's drug reduced lesion counts 56-58%, a figure that would have looked active in isolation; the trial failed because placebo patients improved 52.5%. HS is a flaring disease, and trials enroll patients at a minimum abscess and nodule count, so many enter near a personal peak and regress toward their mean regardless of treatment. Percent change in lesion count, Zura's primary endpoint, is the measure most exposed to this because it has no responder threshold. HiSCR50 raises the bar slightly (UCB's Phase 3 placebo rates were 29-32% at week 16) and HiSCR75 raises it further, which is why newer programs lead with HiSCR75. The second driver is rescue antibiotics: if a protocol allows them and counts those patients as observed, placebo response inflates; if it imputes them as non-responders, placebo response drops but so does the active arm, and the choice of estimand can swing a result from win to miss. That is what happened to sonelokimab in VELA-2 in 2025, where the handling of intercurrent events separated a hit from a miss on the same patients (background from memory, not re-sourced today). Before you trust an HS readout, check four things: the endpoint (HiSCR75 beats percent change), the entry lesion threshold, the rescue-antibiotic rule and how rescued patients are imputed, and the placebo rate against the 16-18% HiSCR75 that UCB saw. A Phase 2 with a 25% placebo-adjusted delta on HiSCR50 under a lenient rescue rule often shrinks to single digits in Phase 3 under a strict one.
