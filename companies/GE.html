Surprise and drivers

The beat came from Revenue, Margin.

Organic
Release gives no organic growth figure. Analysis: no acquisitions closed in the period (CPP not yet closed), so reported growth of +18% adjusted revenue is close to organic.
Margin
Operating margin 22.5%, +220 bps yoy. CES margin -10 bps from mix and inflation, offset by services volume and price. DPT margin +20 bps from volume and price, partly offset by mix and inflation. Adjusted corporate cost and eliminations improved $152M yoy to $(373)M. Source: 3Q26 release. Analysis: about $150M of the $689M yoy operating profit increase came from lower corporate cost and eliminations rather than segments; segments alone grew about 23%.
One-time
Excluded from adjusted results: run-off insurance pretax loss of $(210)M (vs +$363M profit in 3Q25) and a $117M pretax gain on retained ownership interests and equity securities. GAAP continuing EPS $2.32 vs adjusted $2.25. Source: 3Q26 release, adjusted net income reconciliation. Analysis: the adjusted number is clean of both; the insurance swing is a GAAP item worth watching, not an operating one.
Cash conversion
Q3 FCF $2.04B on adjusted net income $2.34B, about 87% conversion; CFOA $2.20B, -14%. Nine months FCF $6.73B, +13%, about 105% conversion. Sources: 3Q26 release; Investing.com transcript (105% YTD).
Share count and tax
Derived diluted shares about 1,041M ($2,343M adjusted net income / $2.25) vs about 1,046M in 2Q26 (A6) and about 1,063M in 3Q25 ($1,764M / $1.66). Adjusted net income +33% vs adjusted EPS +36%. FY26 tax rate about 16% (call, per Investing.com transcript). Analysis: share count added about 3 points of yoy EPS growth; management cited 'previously announced capital allocation actions' but gave no buyback amount.
Segments
CES: revenue $10.34B +21%, services +17%, equipment +38% (engine deliveries +8%, LEAP deliveries +5%, GEnx growth; GE9X shipments not recognized until mid-seal certification), profit $2.89B +21%, margin 27.9% (-10 bps yoy on mix and inflation). DPT: revenue $3.47B +7% (Defense & Systems +5%, Propulsion & Additive +10%), profit $475M +8%, margin 13.7% +20 bps. Corporate cost and eliminations $(373)M vs $(525)M a year ago. Source: 3Q26 release.

2027: commercial services low double digits (about $3.5B to $4B), services margin flat, DPT high single digits, total margin in line with 2026, GE9X about $300M headwind. CFM56 retirements 1.5% to 2.0% in 2026. LEAP shop visits about +40% in Q3. GEnx deliveries up more than 50 units in Q3. GE9X shipping with upgraded mid-seal since August, FAA certification targeted for end 2026. Source: Investing.com transcript.
Nobody asked
Analysis, from the transcript summary rather than a full read: no question on why Q3 commercial services growth (+17%) ran below the FY low-20s guide, on the Q3 FCF decline (-14%), on the $210M insurance loss, or on CPP debt and buyback pace.
Our questions
Q1 (2027 services): answered. Low double digits, about $3.5B to $4B, with LEAP shop visits +25% to 26% and CFM56 visits flat to slightly up; CFM56 retirements 1.5% to 2.0% this year with a step-up expected. What would take it to high single digits was not addressed directly; the lengthening order conversion window and delinquency are the stated soft spots. Q2 (CES margin): partly answered by the print. CES margin 27.9% in Q3 vs 27.3% in Q2, still -10 bps yoy; no 2026 exit rate given, and the CES guide implies Q4 profit of $2.60B to $2.70B, below Q3. Q3 (CPP and buybacks): not answered. No buyback figures; Culp said GE has 'no intention of being a captive provider'; nothing on shop-visit turn times.
