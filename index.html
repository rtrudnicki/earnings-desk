The read

The swing. Governor Cook's speech at 11:15 CT: a nod to a half-point hike on December 9 would undo the relief from the 3.7% print, and a mention of tighter financial conditions after the selloff would extend it.

  • University of Michigan year-ahead inflation expectations 3.7% against 4.3% expected and 4.7% in October; 5-year 3.3% against 3.4%.
  • Headline sentiment in line at 51.5, but Current Economic Conditions missed at 49.4 against 51.6, and interviews ended November 16, before the worst of the selloff.
  • Treasury yields lower: 10-year near 4.95% and 2-year 4.653% early Friday, each down more than 10 basis points, as growth worries took over from hike worries.
  • Technology is still the weak spot: Nasdaq 100 down 1.13% while the S&P 500 turned positive after the 9:00 CT data; Anthropic fell about 9% to 10% the day after its debut.
  • Markets still price a quarter-point hike on December 9 at about 99%, so the open question is size, and Waller's opening remarks this morning added nothing.

Pay attention to

  1. Governor Cook at 11:15 CT, because it is the only policy speech left before the weekend and the first Fed reaction to the 3.7% inflation expectations print.
  2. Whether the S&P 500 holds positive into the afternoon; the 7,237.85 to 7,259.79 zone is the level buyers need to reclaim, with 7,079.91 as the next support.
  3. The 2-year Treasury yield near 4.65%, because a further fall says the market is taking the half-point hike off the table.
  4. The Nasdaq 100 against 26,098, the 61.8% retracement level, and Anthropic against its $83 offering price, as the test of whether the technology selling is done.
  5. Baker Hughes rig count at 12:00 CT (prior 463 oil rigs), an energy item only.
