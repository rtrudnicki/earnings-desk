The read

The swing. The FOMC minutes at 13:00 CT: a broad group leaning toward hiking, or talk of more than one hike, would push the 10-year above 5.10% and undo the bounce.

  • Nvidia: revenue about $115.8B (up 103% y/y), next-quarter guide $126B-$134B above estimates, next-fiscal-year growth target raised to 75% from 70%; shares up about 4% premarket.
  • Futures in snapshots taken between 04:49 and 05:00 CT: S&P 500 +0.5% to +0.7%, Nasdaq-100 +0.9% to +1.2%, Dow +0.3%.
  • Starting point is oversold: S&P 500 fell 1.0% Tuesday to 7,340.53, its lowest close since July 29 and fourth straight loss; Dow -1.6%; VIX 24.18.
  • Rates are the headwind: 10-year 5.10%, 30-year 5.44%, a December 8-9 hike of 25 bp priced at about 96% (CME FedWatch as cited by Reuters), Fed funds at 3.75%-4.00%.
  • Housing data this morning (starts consensus 1.27M, pending sales -2.1%) is second-tier next to the minutes, the auction and retail earnings from Target, Lowe's and TJX.

Pay attention to

  1. FOMC minutes at 13:00 CT: count how many participants saw a case to hike in October beyond Cook and Hammack, and whether anyone discussed moves after December.
  2. The 20-year bond auction at 12:00 CT (prior 5.469%): a weak result with the 10-year at 5.10% would hit stocks before the minutes do.
  3. Whether the Nvidia gap holds through the first hour; the S&P 500 has fallen four days running and sits at its lowest close since July 29.
  4. Target, Lowe's and TJX results before the open for the consumer and housing read, alongside housing starts (consensus 1.27M) at 07:30 CT and pending home sales (consensus -2.1%) at 09:00 CT.
  5. Williams at 13:30 CT, right after the minutes, for any pushback on pricing of a second hike in January.
