Products

The DOE authorised an emergency exchange of up to 400,000 barrels from the SPR (lowest since 1982) for Phillips 66's Lake Charles refinery. Data centers

Take: Capex direction is still up (Meta's floor raised to $135B, hyperscalers tracking ~$800B for 2026) but the equity market stopped rewarding it this week, and the financing side is the next stress: Meta ran negative free cash flow with $83.7B of debt. Power deal
NiSource signs Microsoft to 1,100 MW over 20 years in northwest Indiana, Microsoft funds the generation
NiSource (16 Oct) agreed to supply about 1,100 MW of generation resources for 20 years to a Microsoft campus in northwest Indiana, service from 2028, through NiSource Generation Company LLC; Microsoft funds all required generation and transmission so costs stay off existing NIPSCO customers. GenCo also contracted 600 MW of battery storage under a tolling agreement with an AGP Group affiliate. Generation type and capex were not disclosed; details promised on the Q3 call.
So what: Read: the template is now 'hyperscaler pays for the plant, utility earns a regulated return on it, ratepayers are ring-fenced'. That is the political answer to the PJM cost debate (data-center load was 9% of PJM wholesale cost through July) and it moves the generation capex onto the hyperscaler's balance sheet at exactly the moment the market started counting their cash burn.
