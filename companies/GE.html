Not written yet: the covering analyst fills this before the call.

Our questions for management

  1. Services growth in 2027: removals up more than 10% in the first half and CFM56 retirements near 3%. Does that add up to double-digit services growth next year, and what would take it down to high single digits?
  2. CES margin fell 160 bps in Q2 on installs, investment and inflation. How much of that reverses in the second half, and what is the CES margin exit rate for 2026?
  3. CPP adds $4.75B of new debt. Does it change the pace of buybacks over the next 12 months, and when does CPP capacity start to shorten shop-visit turn times?
