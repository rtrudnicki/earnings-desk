KARD
KATALYST-AV topline slips to 2H 2027 as Kardigan moves the primary calcium endpoint to Week 48
Friday Nov 6 Kardigan said Phase 2b KATALYST-AV (ataciguat, aortic stenosis) completed enrollment at 156 patients and that the protocol was amended, blinded and not for safety, to assess the primary endpoint (aortic valve calcium, AVC) at Week 48 instead of the 24-week interim it had guided for 1H 2027. Week 24 becomes an internal look; Week 48 topline is now 2H 2027 and a Phase 3 design update comes 1H 2027. Baseline AVC in the first 139 patients ranged 320 to 1,983 Agatston units, and a 45,000-patient retrospective registry analysis showed baseline aortic valve area, not AVC, separated rapid progressors (27% losing at least 0.1 cm2/yr) from the rest. Investor webcast ran Saturday 8:00 ET from AHA.
Why it matters: This is a six-month slip on the only near-term ataciguat catalyst, presented as endpoint science. The company's own data say the surrogate it is powering on (calcium) does not track the pace of disease, which is the question a Phase 3 and the FDA will ask. Danicamtiv and tonlamarsen readouts remain the other legs; the IPO priced at $16 in June and the stock sat near $14.50 in late September, so the name has no cushion for a second move. His catalyst table already carries the new 2027-06-30 Phase 3 plan row; the 2H 2027 topline should replace the 1H 2027 date wherever he has it.

One level deeper

Target engagement is not an outcome: what Lp(a)HORIZON, ZEUS and Kardigan's endpoint move have in common
Analysis, not news. Two drugs did exactly what they were built to do this weekend and changed nothing: pelacarsen cut Lp(a) 78% (236 to 64 nmol/L) for an event hazard ratio of 1.02, and ziltivekimab cut free IL-6 by 76 percentage points for 0.99. A surrogate earns the right to stand in for outcomes only when two things hold: the marker sits on the causal path, and the drug's effect on outcomes runs entirely through its effect on the marker (the Prentice conditions). Mendelian randomization supports the first for Lp(a) but says nothing about the second, and it estimates lifetime exposure in whole populations, not four years of lowering in 65-year-olds with established plaque; the MR models themselves implied roughly a 100 mg/dL (about 215-250 nmol/L) absolute reduction for a 20% event cut, and the trial delivered about 172 nmol/L, so the dose-of-effect question was live before the first patient enrolled. IL-6 is one step downstream of the IL-1 beta signal that worked in CANTOS (HR 0.85), and ZEUS shows that a pathway win does not transfer between nodes, and that the on-target cost (serious infections) arrives whether or not the benefit does. Now read Kardigan's Friday release with that lens: the company moved its primary endpoint, aortic valve calcium, to Week 48 because baseline calcium varied sixfold, and its own 45,000-patient dataset showed calcium does not separate fast progressors from slow ones while valve area does. A trial powered to win on calcium can win the biomarker and lose the clinic, which is why the Phase 3 design update in 1H 2027 matters more than the 2H 2027 Phase 2b topline. The working rule for his notes: for any program whose next catalyst is a biomarker, write down whether that biomarker has ever been validated against hard outcomes in a randomized trial of a drug with the same mechanism; weight loss has SELECT, LDL has decades of statin and PCSK9 data, Lp(a) and IL-6 had genetics, and valve calcium has neither.
