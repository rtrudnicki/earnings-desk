OPEC
Core seven held December targets; the 2027 framework comes Nov 29
At the Nov 1 virtual meeting the seven members kept quotas at September levels (Saudi 10.5 mb/d, Russia 9.9, Iraq 4.4, Kuwait 2.7) and said they will keep meeting monthly. Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Iraq remain well below quota because of Strait of Hormuz constraints, and about 2 mb/d of cuts still cover most members through end-2026. The UAE left OPEC in May.
So what: If Hormuz reopens, the binding constraint becomes the quota ceiling rather than the cut, so Gulf barrels return on top of any risk-premium unwind. Read: the Nov 29 meeting will set 2027 supply into whatever the Iran talks have done to price, which makes it the next real oil event after the ceasefire headlines.
