Microsoft confirmed it has left the Loughton, Essex arrangement, calling it a single-site decision; the facility was meant to supply about 50 MW of AI compute from 2027, and grid officials reportedly told Nscale power would not arrive until the early-to-mid 2030s. The Telegraph says more than 40% of Nscale's $103B contracted revenue comes from Microsoft; Nscale is preparing an NYSE IPO. Reported Oct 18-19.
So what: A hyperscaler will now drop a site rather than wait on a grid queue, which transfers the interconnection risk onto the neocloud and its lenders.
