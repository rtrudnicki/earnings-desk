Company overview Thomas 2026-10-16

Harbor Logistics runs 41 inland container terminals and a truck brokerage; it sells throughput to shippers and rail partners under 3 to 7 year take-or-pay contracts.

How it makes money: Terminal fees 58%, brokerage 27%, storage and chassis 15%.

Bull

Contracted volumes step up 9% in FY27 as two Gulf terminals open; pricing resets on 40% of the book; buybacks retire 4% of shares a year.

Bear

Brokerage margins compress when truck capacity loosens; Gulf ramp slips a quarter; leverage at 3.1x limits the buyback.
