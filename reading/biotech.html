VMT-alpha-NET ESMO rapid oral: 50% ORR at 6.0 mCi, 72% PFS at 18 months, Phase 3 start now 2027
Perspective presented Cohorts 1 to 3 of the Phase 1/2a trial of [212Pb]bamzireotide navoxetan (VMT-alpha-NET) in 68 PRRT-naive SSTR2+ NET patients at ESMO on October 23 (data cutoff September 2, 2026; response counts updated September 10). One level deeper

How to read a small-cohort radioligand ORR: the four choices that moved CATX's headline numbers
Perspective's 50% ORR at 6.0 mCi is built from four decisions an analyst should unpack before comparing it to Lutathera. First, the denominator: the release reports ORR on all treated patients (10/20) and on a subgroup with SSTR2 expression in every target lesion (8/17, 47%); the subgroup is the biologically cleaner number because a non-expressing lesion cannot respond to a targeted emitter, and the Phase 3 will enroll on that basis. Second, confirmation: ORR 'includes unconfirmed responses,' and two of the ten Cohort 3 responses were unconfirmed as of September 10, so the confirmed rate is 8/20 (40%); unconfirmed PRs in NETs often hold because the disease is indolent, but they also often fall back inside the 30% threshold on the next scan. Third, the cutoff: efficacy was analyzed at a September 2 cutoff but response counts were refreshed to September 10, which is legitimate and disclosed, yet it means the ORR and the PFS curve are not from the same snapshot. Fourth, the PFS presentation: 72% at 18 months is a landmark rate on a pooled 68-patient population across three doses with no median, and a landmark is flattered when follow-up is short because the at-risk set at 18 months is a small fraction of enrollees. Against that, NETTER-1's 18% ORR came from second-line G1/G2 midgut NETs after progression on octreotide, and NETTER-2's 43% came from first-line higher-grade (G2/G3) disease, so neither matches a PRRT-naive mixed cohort. The honest comparison is within-trial: a dose-response from 33% to 50% between 5.0 and 6.0 mCi with no added grade 4 toxicity is the finding that supports the Phase 3 dose, and the 2.9% grade 3 renal rate is the number that decides whether FDA asks for dosimetry-based individualization before approval.
